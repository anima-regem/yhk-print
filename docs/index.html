Print through your Mac

The Mac relay connects to the printer over Bluetooth.

On your Mac, run ./yhk web --no-open and open the local print app.

On your phone, join the same Wi-Fi as your Mac. Run ./yhk web --share --no-open on the Mac and open the address it shows on your phone.

Connection and installation help

On Android, open this page in Chrome and tap Connect printer. Choose Install app in Chrome's menu to add it to your home screen.

On iPhone, open this page in Bluefy for direct Bluetooth.

In Firefox or Safari, use the Mac relay.

Keep the printer on and nearby. Disconnect it from other printing apps before connecting here.
